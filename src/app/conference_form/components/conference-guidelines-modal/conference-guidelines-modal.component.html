Guidelines for Applicants

A. Guidelines for applicants:

Percentage of funding support for presenting or participating in online or physical conferences will use the following guidelines with the exception that budget from external fund where the following limitations will not be applied to:

Each academic is allowed one international and one local conference per year including APU organised conference and the applicant must be the same presenter in the conference.

Sponsorship for paper presentation at conference within Malaysia:

Local Venue: Throughout Malaysia
Percentage: A maximum of 85% & limit to a maximum of RM5,000, whichever is lower

Sponsorship for paper presentation at overseas conference is based on region:

Region: ASEAN Countries
Percentage: A maximum of 85% & limit to a maximum of RM5,000, whichever is lower
Region: Australasia (Australia, New Zealand, PNG, Timor Leste)
Percentage: A maximum of 70% & limit to a maximum of RM5,000, whichever is lower
Region: Asia (Japan, China, Korea, Central Asia, India, Sri Lanka, Pakistan)
Percentage: A maximum of 70% & limit to a maximum of RM5,000, whichever is lower
Region: Pacific Islands (Maldives etc.)
Percentage: A maximum of 70% & limit to a maximum of RM5,000, whichever is lower
Region: Europe
Percentage: A maximum of 50% & limit to a maximum of RM5,000, whichever is lower
Region: The Americas (USA, Canada, Mexico and other parts of South America)
Percentage: A maximum of 50% & limit to a maximum of RM5,000, whichever is lower
Region: Middle East and Africa
Percentage: A maximum of 60% & limit to a maximum of RM5,000, whichever is lower

Sponsorship for attendance at local conference (without paper presentation):

The University recognises that conferences are a major source of cutting-edge research, and academic staff may apply to attend conferences without giving a paper presentation. However, this is subject to the discretion of the management and limited to conferences held locally, where overnight stay is not required. The University will only consider the conference participation fee as stated below. It is to be clearly understood that there shall not be any other claim.

Cost: ≤ RM250.00 Percentage: 100%
Cost: RM251.00 - RM700.00 Percentage: 60%
Cost: ≥RM701.00 - RM1,500.00 Percentage: 40%
Cost: ≥RM1,500.00 Percentage: 20%

The sponsorship to be considered for presentation in conferences held overseas and within Malaysia will only include the conference fee, journal publication fee, accommodation based on 1 day before and 1 day after, airfare, and ground transportation such as taxi, train and bus, where applicable. It is to be clearly understood that staff will not be eligible for any form of allowances such as meal allowance and staff allowance. If the conference is held in Klang Valley, transportation claim is not allowed where staff can request for university transport. Accommodation allowed is 3-star and below.

Resigned staff, while serving notice and under probation, will not be sponsored to present or participate in any conference.

Conference proceedings must be published in SCOPUS-indexed.

B. Conference presentation funded by external fund

Conference presentation funded by external fund requires to comply with the fund requirements including the type of expenses allowable (refer to Appendix 1 for details).

C. Payment for conference presentation or participation costs

Expenses relating to conference presentation or participation (e.g. conference fee, flight ticket, accommodation, and transport) to be pay and claim upon return from conference. Claim Form with original receipts to be submitted within 7 days upon return from conference. Staff can request for advance money by completing the Advance Requisition Voucher. Finance Department will provide 50% of the amount approved in the Funding Request for Conference form.

D. Applicants need to take note of the following

In the case of joint authorship with author(s) in another higher education institution, government agency, and/or company, the costs of attending conference is highly encouraged to be borne jointly. Joint publications with international and/or local Higher Education Institutions and/or industry is highly encouraged. Please refer to section 5.3 for details relating to authorship. 'Travel Authorisation Form' needs to be completed, approved and be included as supporting document for funding support for conference presentation out-of-campus.

E. The application process for funding support

The application process for funding support for conference presentations is as follows:

  • Applicant submits the 'Funding Request for Conference Presentation' application form to his/her Head of School
  • Applicant to submit the form together with the evidence including print screen information on Scopus-indexed publication, reviewers' comments, final draft conference, matching grant (if any), 'Travel Authorisation' form (if relevant) and acceptance notification
  • Head of School must review and support the funding request before submitting the form and supporting documents to RMC for checking
  • RMC will forward the checked form and documents to Vice Chancellor for approval then will be forwarded to Human Resource for processing

F. The review and approval process

The review and approval process for funding support for conference presentation is as follows:

  • Head of Schools to verify if the conference proceedings are indexed in SCOPUS
  • A copy of the received completed application form and supporting documents need to be send to the RMC for checking follow by Vice Chancellor for approval, then to Human Resources for sponsorship amount verification and processing
  • Human Resource Department will inform the applicant whether the application is successful or not and the approved amount
  • It is the applicant's responsibility to ensure the requirements are completed upon returning from any conference and after paper presented at conference is published in the conference proceedings that is indexed in SCOPUS

G. Duties expected of staff upon returning from a conference

The following are duties expected of staff upon returning from a conference, and/or publication of paper in conference proceedings:

  • Submit evidence of conference proceedings indexed in SCOPUS to the monthly return portal for retention and record within a month's return from conference or after publication is published
  • Academic needs to present in a sharing session within 3 months of publication or upon return from conference at the School level
  • Failure to comply with the above-mentioned within the stipulated time, and unless good reason(s) are given in writing, the staff concerned is liable to reimburse the full amount of costs incurred in attending the conference. The amount sponsored will then be deducted from his/her monthly salary, and the outstanding amount in full upon resignation from APU
