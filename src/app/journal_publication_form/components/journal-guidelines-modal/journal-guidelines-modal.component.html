Journal Publication Guidelines

A. Guidelines for applicants:

APC sponsorship will be awarded based on the following criteria:

Author Requirements:

  • The applicant must be the first author or corresponding author of the article and must be affiliated with APU.
  • The article must have at least one co-author from a country as indicated in the International Research Network (IRN) map.

Collaboration Priority:

International collaborations and joint publications with partners from industry and/or national agencies will be prioritized for sponsorship, particularly those that align with APU's strategic research goals.

B. The different tiers in APC sponsorship are based on the impact and collaboration type of the publication:

100% Sponsorship for:

  • SCOPUS-indexed journal listed in Quartile Q1 or Q2; and
  • Must have at least one international co-author from a country on the IRN map or one industrial co-author.

50% Sponsorship for:

  • SCOPUS-indexed journal listed in Quartile Q3; and
  • Articles that involve international and/or industry co-author and/or co-author from another Malaysian higher education institution.

APC sponsorship exceeding RM 10,000 will require pre-approval from Vice Chancellor.

C. Applicants need to take note of the following:

  • While this policy outlines which journal tiers are eligible for sponsorship, this does not restrict staff from publishing. Staff are free to choose journals that do not require APC or to publish work in journals with unsupported APCs at their own cost and responsibility.
  • All publications must have APU affiliation in the situation where author is still an APU staff member or resigned staff where content of the research/publication work was developed while at APU.
  • Journal publication fee will be paid directly to publisher either through bank transfer or APU credit card. Applicant to provide payment details to Finance.
  • Content of the journal publications must not mostly been published previously in any other media, journal, conference proceedings, book, etc.
  • Please refer to section 5.3 for details relating to authorship.

D. The application process for APC sponsorship is as follows:

  1. Applicant submits the ‘Sponsorship Request for Journal Publication’ application form to his/her Head of School.
  2. Applicant to submit the form together with the evidence including print screen information on Scopus-indexed journal, reviewers’ comments, final draft journal publication, matching grant (if any), Turnitin report (if relevant) and acceptance notification.
  3. Head of School must review and support the funding request before submitting the form and supporting documents to RMC for checking.
  4. RMC will forward the checked form and documents to Vice Chancellor for approval then will be forwarded to Human Resource and Finance for processing.

E. The review and approval process:

  • Head of Schools to verify if the journal publications are indexed in SCOPUS.
  • A copy of the received completed application form and supporting documents need to be send to the RMC for checking follow by Vice Chancellor for approval, then to Human Resources for sponsorship amount verification and processing.
  • Human Resource Department will inform the applicant whether the application is successful or not and the approved amount.

It is the applicant’s responsibility to ensure that if the approved APC sponsorship for a journal publication is rejected but APC paid to the journal, he/she needs to submit the article to another SCOPUS-indexed journal that do not require APC or personally pay the new APC.

F. Duties expected of staff upon publication:

  • Submit evidence of article published in journal indexed in SCOPUS to the monthly return portal for retention and record within a month’s after publication is published.
  • Failure to publish after APC is paid and unless good reason(s) are given in writing, the staff concerned is liable to reimburse the full amount of costs incurred in APC sponsorship. The amount sponsored will then be deducted from his/her monthly salary, and the outstanding amount in full upon resignation from APU.
